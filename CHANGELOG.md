# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-05

- Add weighted multivariate Silverman Gaussian KDEs in JAX, with chunked stable
  log-density evaluation and inverse-CDF mixture resampling.
- Add KDE total-correlation scoring and faithful JAX Seeded Greedy Maximization.
- Add normalized Morph product states, independent proposals, and singleton factors.
- Add sample-split MorphZ optimal bridge sampling in log space, with convergence
  history, overlap, importance ESS, and autocorrelation-adjusted relative error.
- Add reference-equivalence and analytical-evidence tests, a Gaussian example,
  a reproducible SGM benchmark, CI, and mathematical/implementation records.
